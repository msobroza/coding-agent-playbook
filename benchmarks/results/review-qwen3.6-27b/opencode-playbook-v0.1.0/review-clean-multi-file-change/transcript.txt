**Design and boundaries:**

- `src/shipping.py:11`, `src/units.py:7`: `unit: str` — a typo like `"lbs"` or `"LB"` compiles fine and only fails at runtime. `Literal["kg", "lb"]` catches the typo at the call site. Principle: make invalid states unrepresentable. Python idiom: `typing.Literal`.

The choice to validate `weight <= 0` in `quote_cents` before delegating to `to_kilograms` is good — it keeps the conversion function a pure calculator and puts the business rule at the boundary. The `from None` on the re-raised `ValueError` is also correct: the `KeyError` traceback from a dict lookup adds no debug value when the message already names the bad unit and the valid options.
