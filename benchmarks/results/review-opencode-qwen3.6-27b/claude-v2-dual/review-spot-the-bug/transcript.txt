* **`src/pagination.py:22`** — `important` — `end = len(items) - 1` is an off-by-one error. The slice `items[start:end]` uses an exclusive upper bound, so clamping to `len(items) - 1` drops the last element of the final page. `paginate(['a', 'b', 'c', 'd', 'e'], page=1, page_size=3)` returns `['d']` instead of `['d', 'e']`. In the worse case, `paginate(['a'], page=0, page_size=5)` clamps `end` to `0`, yielding `[]` instead of `['a']` — the entire list is lost.
