- **src/auth.py:16-17** — `(!hmac.compare_digest(...) or ...)` — `important` — Short-circuit evaluation of `or` defeats the constant-time guarantee. If the username mismatches, `hmac.compare_digest` on the password is never called, so timing still leaks whether the username was correct. Even after fixing `!` → `not`, the same short-circuit applies. Evaluate both comparisons unconditionally first (store each in a variable), then combine the booleans.
