Review complete. Found and fixed one bug:

**Off-by-one** — `src/pagination.py:22` clamped `end` to `len(items) - 1` instead of `len(items)`, silently dropping the last item whenever a page extended past the end of the list (e.g. the final page of `["a".."e"]` with `page_size=2` returned `[]` instead of `["e"]`). Fixed to `end = len(items)` and verified with edge cases (exact-fit page, partial last page, mid-list page, empty list) on Python 3.12.
